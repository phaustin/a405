[6)] (12) Mixing¶

Surface air at 1000 hPa with a temperature of 20 deg C and a dewpoint of 16 deg C is lifted adiabatically to 800 hPa, where it entrains 70% of environmental air that has a θe\theta_eθe​ of 307 K and a mixing ratio of 4 g/kg. Use the tephigram labeled ``mixing problem’’ to find:

  • The θe\theta_eθe​ and LCL of the of the surface air

  • The θe\theta_eθe​, rvr_vrv​ and rlr_lrl​ of the mixture

  • The LCL of the mixture

  • The temperature of the mixture at 800 hPa

Is the mixture negatively, positively, or neutrally buoyant with its surrounding environment? Explain.
